## Overall Impression

Hero lands well and Photography is the strongest section. The contact end is transactional and the middle has a valley (About, Skills).

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Contact form promises a send but opens a mail draft.** Contact.vue button "Send message"; submit sets window.location to mailto and shows "Opening your email client…". Visitors on webmail get nothing. Fix: rename the button to "Open email draft", show the address as a mailto link if nothing opened, keep field values. Hero "Get in touch" should point to #contact. Command: clarify, then harden.
2. **[P1] Years and projects are buried.** About.vue line 10 renders "8+ years of experience · 25+ projects" in small muted text. Fix: surface them in the hero as stats using font-display and text-lead. Command: layout.
3. **[P1] About section has no heading.** About.vue has no h2; screen-reader heading navigation skips it. Fix: add an h2 matching Skills. Command: harden.
4. **[P2] Skills cards put labels last; grid leaves a hole; keywords wrap.** Skills.vue lines 9–12. Fix: label above list, wrap items, reflow to fill the grid. Command: layout.
5. **[P2] Touch targets about 40px.** Navbar hamburger and Photography viewer buttons use p-2. Fix: p-3 (48px); raise close-button contrast. Command: polish.
